Privacy Policy

App: SAT Prep Pulse  |  Last updated: September 2026

Overview

SAT Prep Pulse is an iOS SAT practice quiz app by Maruti Kampli. Your privacy is important to us.

What We Do Not Collect

SAT Prep Pulse does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in required
  • No name, email, or contact info collected
  • No location data
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs
  • No usage data sent to external servers

Data on Your Device

Practice scores and progress are stored locally on your device only using Apple iOS storage APIs. This data is never transmitted and is inaccessible to us.

In-App Purchases

The one-time Lifetime Access purchase is processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive or store payment information.

Children

The App does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
