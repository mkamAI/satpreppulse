Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Lifetime Access purchase?

Open SAT Prep Pulse, tap the unlock button, and then tap "Restore Purchase." This restores your purchase on the same Apple ID at no additional charge.

Can I use the app on multiple devices?

Yes. The Lifetime Access in-app purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. SAT Prep Pulse requires no account or sign-in. All your progress is stored locally on your device.

What SAT topics are covered?

The app includes 50+ questions covering Math, Reading, and Writing & Language sections of the SAT.

How do I report a bug or inaccurate question?

Email us at maruti.kampli@gmail.com with details and we will address it promptly.
